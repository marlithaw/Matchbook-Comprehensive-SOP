A Personalized, Competency-Based Education

Core subjects are taught with high-quality, standards-aligned materials (Savvas MyView, enVision Math, MyPerspectives). Students advance by demonstrating mastery, not a fixed pace. Five competencies shape the proficiency scale and each student's monthly conference:

  • Academic Ownership — I take charge of my own learning.
  • Collaboration — I work well with others toward a shared goal.
  • Communication — I share my ideas clearly and listen to others.
  • Honor — I take responsibility and treat my community with respect.
  • Resilience — I keep trying and use feedback to get better.

Quick Family Reference

Attendance help → call the main office by 9:00 AM. Academic progress → PowerSchool + classroom teacher. Behavior/support question → start with the teacher. Counseling or wellness → counselor, social worker, or main office. Title IX/harassment concern → main office or titleix@matchbooklearning.com. Emergency pickup → call the main office immediately.

Matchbook students are transported via IPS / First Student. Riding the bus is a privilege: obey the driver, stay seated, keep hands and feet to yourself, no food or drinks, and arrive about 10 minutes early for pickup.

Section 05

Attendance & Chronic-Absence Supports

Indiana law requires school attendance for all students. Report all absences to the main office by 9:00 AM; a written note or physician documentation is required for an excused absence upon return.

Tardiness

Sign in at the main office after 7:35 AM. Excessive tardiness is addressed through MTSS and parent conferences.

Chronic Absenteeism

Missing 10% or more of school days. Matchbook works actively with families to remove barriers.

Section 08

Conduct, Dress & Technology

Matchbook is a non-violent, non-uniform community. When in doubt about dress: would my Head of School be comfortable seeing me in this?

Harassment, Intimidation & Bullying (HIB)

Bullying — including cyberbullying — results in consequences aligned with the Student Code of Conduct. Report any incident to a teacher, counselor, or administrator.

Cell Phone Policy — Simple & Firm

Personal electronics may not be used, visible, or accessible during the school day. Devices are powered off and stored the entire day. To reach a student urgently, call the main office at (317) 226-4263.

Every student has a school-issued 1:1 device. A Technology Agreement must be signed before issuance; devices stay at school and are used for educational purposes only.

Section 09

Student Code of Conduct

Matchbook is a restorative school community. Restorative practice does not mean there are no consequences — it means consequences help students understand impact, take responsibility, repair when possible, and learn the next right step.

How Matchbook Responds To Behavior
LevelPossible Matchbook Response
Level 1Reteaching, redirection, restorative conversation, family communication if patterns continue.
Level 2Family partnership, counselor/dean/restorative support, MTSS review, DBRC/CICO.
Level 3Administrator involvement, formal documentation, restorative re-entry plan, possible OSS.
Level 4Immediate administrative review, due process, possible suspension or expulsion referral.

No Student Sent Home Without Family Contact

No student is dismissed early or removed for disciplinary reasons without parent/guardian contact and administrative authorization, except in an emergency.

Students with disabilities retain all protections under IDEA, Section 504, and Indiana Article 7 — nothing in this handbook limits those rights.

Anti-Harassment, Non-Discrimination & Title IX

Matchbook prohibits sex-based discrimination in compliance with Title IX. Report concerns to the main office at (317) 226-4263 or titleix@matchbooklearning.com.

Student Agreement

As a Matchbook Learning Cardinal, I commit to:

  • Coming to school every day, on time, ready to learn
  • Treating every person in this building with respect
  • Following the dress code and keeping personal electronics powered off all day
  • Never using physical aggression, threats, or harassment toward anyone
  • Reporting bullying, threats, or unsafe situations to a staff member

Parent / Guardian Agreement

As a Matchbook Learning family, we commit to:

  • Ensuring our child arrives ready to learn by 7:35 AM every day
  • Reading all school communications and keeping contact information current
  • Monitoring our child's progress through PowerSchool regularly
  • Supporting school consequences and attending a reinstatement meeting after any suspension
